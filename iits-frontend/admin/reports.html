PAJSK year-end export

Generate the final PAJSK dataset for submission — no manual re-entry into the government portal.

Includes role points, achievement points, attendance points, NILAM points, and the total for every student — all calculated automatically.

What gets exported

  • Student number, name, and class
  • AJK role points (e.g. President, Secretary)
  • Competition achievement points
  • Attendance points (present / late / excused, by config weighting)
  • NILAM reading points
  • Total PAJSK score
