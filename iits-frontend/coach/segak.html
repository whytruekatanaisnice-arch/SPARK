Test recording guide

Step test: record recovery pulse in beats/minute after the 3-minute test (96 BPM cadence). Push-ups and curl-ups: record completed repetitions in 1 minute. Select the push-up method used. Sit-and-reach is recorded in centimetres.

BMI is calculated as weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². This page reports the numeric BMI only; interpretation for students should use the school’s age- and sex-specific guidance.
